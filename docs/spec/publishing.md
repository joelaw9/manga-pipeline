# manga_pipeline spec — Publishing

Part of the spec; the index is [`SPEC.md`](../../SPEC.md). Update this file in the same change as the behaviour it describes.

## Publishing

The finished CBZ is written to a staging folder on the library's NFS export, outside every Kavita library root, then renamed into the series folder. Its `ComicInfo.xml` `Series` is the series' name from its bulk-converter record, which must be one of the series' Kavita names under Kavita's normalising (SPEC "State"), and its `LocalizedSeries` is Kavita's localized name (left out when Kavita has none). The rest of the download's `ComicInfo.xml` is kept where each field is valid for the schema, except what the pipeline writes itself (SPEC "Metadata", ComicInfo); `Number` comes from the chapter's name where the download has none, or no `ComicInfo.xml` at all. `Title` and `Volume` are the bulk converter's: the title is the download's `Title` without a leading label of the chapter's own number ("Chapter 4 : Where Is She?" → "Where Is She?"), else the name's title, with status markers (`[END]`, `(End of Season 1)`, …) stripped, and "Chapter <number>" when none is left (recorded as no title, as the baseline records an untitled bulk chapter, so the 2 compare equal); `Volume` is the chapter's volume below, and is left out when it has none.

A new series gets its own folder directly under the library root: `<library root>/<series>`. A chapter of a series Kavita already knows goes into the series folder: the single folder that holds the series' files (SPEC "Services"), which must exist. Before a run publishes into a series Kavita holds, the folder Kavita's files of the series lie in must be the series' recorded folder; otherwise the series is `needs_human`, naming both, and nothing is published into it until a person links it again (`series link`, which records Kavita's folder) and retries it. A chapter is renamed into the series' folder as it is when it is published. The pipeline never creates a second folder for a series, and it does not move series folders.

A file the pipeline publishes is named as the bulk converter names its files: `Vol.<volume> Ch.<number> - <title>.cbz`, without `Vol.<volume> ` when the chapter has no volume and without ` - <title>` when it has no title or none is left once made safe. The title is made safe as the bulk converter does: ": " becomes " - ", the characters `\ / : * ? " < > |` become `_`, trailing dots and spaces go, and it is cut to 80 characters ending in "…".

A new chapter is named as the bulk converter names a chapter next to the series' recorded ones, with every published file and every open chapter of the series in view (chapters that arrive together see each other):

- Volume of a main chapter, from the source the series' bulk-converter record names as its volume basis (a new series' basis is chosen once, at validate, when none of its chapters is still at discover, over its chapters known then, by the bulk converter's order for a series it meets first: the first of `filename` (any main chapter's own volume), `season tags` (every chapter carries its season), `season markers` (a season-end marker), `mangadex` (MangaDex volumes 50 % or more of the main chapters), `override` (the override file volumes a main chapter), the side-story default (side stories and no main volume), else `none`): `filename` → the file's own volume (`Vol.N` in its name, else its `ComicInfo.xml` `Volume`); `season tags` → its season tag; `season markers` → its season, read from season-end and season-start markers in the chapters' titles (`ComicInfo.xml` `Title`, else the download's name) and from the record's own season volumes (each but the highest ends at its highest chapter; the highest stays open until a marker closes it), volumes numbered 1, 2, … in season order and the next one after the last end; `mangadex` → MangaDex's volume of its number (the stored aggregate); `override` → the series' override file `<paths.naming_overrides>/<series rules key>.yaml` (the name of the series' first folder, SPEC "Series identification"; `volumes: {1: "1-65", 2: "66-"}`; a chapter in no range has no volume); the side-story default → 1. The bases `none`, `tapas seasons` and `webtoons seasons` give no volume (an official platform's episode list is not fetched yet).
- Gap fill, for the `filename`, `mangadex` and `none` bases: a main chapter the basis gives no volume takes the override file's volume (a `none` series whose override file re-volumed its recorded chapters follows it), then (`filename` only) MangaDex's, each from a source only when it gives the same volume as the basis on every chapter both cover, and only where the volume fits between its neighbours' (volumes never decrease along the chapter numbers); a source whose own fills are out of order fills nothing.
- A side story goes in the volume the series' side stories already have, else the volume after the highest main volume, else 2; side stories are numbered on from the highest published one, in the order (volume in its name, number, date, arrival); its title is `Side Story <n>: <title>` (the side tag and a bare chapter label stripped), or `Side Story <n>` when none is left.
- A volume that is not a whole number (`ComicInfo.xml` `Volume` is an integer), from any source, a missing or malformed override file of an `override` series, and a malformed override file of any series make the chapter `needs_human` (at the stage that names it: validate's checks, or comicinfo); a series with no override file and another basis has no override.
- An untitled chapter keeps "Chapter <number>"; titles from the sources are not taken.

A published file whose chapter's volume later differs from the volume its name holds (none included) is renamed, with reading progress carried; a file the bulk converter named is renamed only when its chapter loses its volume altogether.

A library file is never overwritten. When the target name is taken, the chapter is `needs_human`, unless the staged file is gone and the target has the staged size: then the rename already happened (a crash after it) and publishing carries on. A published file recorded at a free target name describes a file that is gone: the new file's record replaces it.

Kavita scans are never requested back to back: a request made while a scan runs does not work. `export` requests no series scans; after all its file work and its confirms it requests at most 1 Kavita library scan, of `kavita.library_id`, when it published a file since the last request or chapters still wait on `kavita_scan`. A scan is awaited while chapters still wait on `kavita_scan` and its request is younger than `kavita.rescan_after_minutes`; while one is awaited no scan is requested, even after a publish: the new files wait for the next request. A request that fails is made by a later run. A chapter waits on `kavita_scan` until Kavita lists its file in the series with the size on disk and the series still exists. When a scan is requested while chapters still wait, each chapter that was already waiting when the previous request was made counts 1 attempt; a chapter past the attempt limit is `needs_human`. A series Kavita no longer holds is `needs_human`; its chapters keep waiting until a person links it again. A new series is published when none of its chapters is still on its way through `intake` or `process` (waiting and `needs_human` chapters aside): all its finished chapters are renamed in together, and the run's library scan indexes them.

Once Kavita has indexed the file, the pipeline removes the CBZ from the downloads folder directly, not through Suwayomi, so Suwayomi keeps the chapter marked downloaded and does not fetch it again. With `export.retention_days` above 0, the CBZ is moved to `<paths.retention>/<source>/<title>/` (outside the downloads folder and every library root; when a file of its name is already there, under the first free name `<stem> (<n>)<suffix>`, n from 2, so both are kept) and touched, and the next `export` after the period deletes it by its modification time; with 0 it is deleted at once. A download already gone counts as removed. Every download the pipeline removes, a put-aside one included, goes the same way. A rejected or failed download (SPEC "Bad downloads") also goes the same way, and then Suwayomi's downloaded mark is cleared through Suwayomi, so the chapter can be downloaded again.
