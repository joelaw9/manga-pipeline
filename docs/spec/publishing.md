# manga_pipeline spec — Publishing

Part of the spec; the index is [`SPEC.md`](../../SPEC.md). Update this file in the same change as the behaviour it describes.

## Publishing

The finished CBZ is written to a staging folder on the library's NFS export, outside every Kavita library root, then renamed into the series folder. Its `ComicInfo.xml` `Series` is the series' name from its bulk-converter record, which must be one of the series' Kavita names under Kavita's normalising (SPEC "State"), and its `LocalizedSeries` is Kavita's localized name (left out when Kavita has none). The rest of the download's `ComicInfo.xml` is kept where each field is valid for the schema; `Number` comes from the chapter's name where the download has none, or no `ComicInfo.xml` at all. `Title` and `Volume` are the bulk converter's: the title is the download's `Title` without a leading label of the chapter's own number ("Chapter 4 : Where Is She?" → "Where Is She?"), else the name's title, with status markers (`[END]`, `(End of Season 1)`, …) stripped, and "Chapter <number>" when none is left; `Volume` is the chapter's volume below, and is left out when it has none.

A new series gets its own folder directly under the library root: `<library root>/<series>`. A chapter of a series Kavita already knows goes into the series folder: the single folder that holds the series' files (SPEC "Services"), which must exist. The pipeline never creates a second folder for a series, and it does not move series folders.

A file the pipeline publishes is named as the bulk converter names its files: `Vol.<volume> Ch.<number> - <title>.cbz`, without `Vol.<volume> ` when the chapter has no volume and without ` - <title>` when it has no title or none is left once made safe. The title is made safe as the bulk converter does: ": " becomes " - ", the characters `\ / : * ? " < > |` become `_`, trailing dots and spaces go, and it is cut to 80 characters ending in "…".

A new chapter's volume comes from the source the series' bulk-converter record names as its volume basis, as the bulk converter decides it for a chapter next to recorded ones: `filename` → the file's own volume (`Vol.N` in its name, else its `ComicInfo.xml` `Volume`); `season tags` → its season tag; `override` → the series' override file `<paths.naming_overrides>/<series folder name>.yaml` (a work folder of the instance, `rules/naming` by default, never part of the repository) (`volumes: {1: "1-65", 2: "66-"}`; a chapter in no range has no volume); the side-story default → 1. Any other basis (season markers, an official platform's seasons, MangaDex), and a side story, gives no volume until the metadata checks resolve it. An override series whose file is missing or malformed, and a file-name volume that is not a whole number (`ComicInfo.xml` `Volume` is an integer), make the chapter `needs_human` at the comicinfo stage at once. A published file whose chapter's volume later differs from the volume its name holds (none included) is renamed, with reading progress carried; a file the bulk converter named is renamed only when its chapter loses its volume altogether.

A library file is never overwritten. When the target name is taken, the chapter is `needs_human`, unless the staged file is gone and the target has the staged size: then the rename already happened (a crash after it) and publishing carries on. A published file recorded at a free target name describes a file that is gone: the new file's record replaces it.

`export` requests 1 Kavita series scan for each series it published into, after publishing its ready chapters; a request that fails is made by the next run. A chapter waits on `kavita_scan` until Kavita lists its file in the series with the size on disk and the series still exists. When chapters of a series still wait `kavita.rescan_after_minutes` after the last request, the scan is requested again and each of them counts 1 attempt; a chapter past the attempt limit is `needs_human`. A series Kavita no longer holds is `needs_human`; its chapters keep waiting until a person links it again. A new series is published when none of its chapters is still on its way through `intake` or `process` (waiting and `needs_human` chapters aside): all its finished chapters are renamed in together and 1 Kavita library scan is requested. Only 1 library scan is awaited at a time; another new series waits its turn. The wait ends when every chapter of the batch is indexed. Once Kavita knows the series, its later chapters use series scans.

Once Kavita has indexed the file, the pipeline removes the CBZ from the downloads folder directly, not through Suwayomi, so Suwayomi keeps the chapter marked downloaded and does not fetch it again. With `export.retention_days` above 0, the CBZ is moved to `<paths.retention>/<source>/<title>/` (outside the downloads folder and every library root) and touched, and the next `export` after the period deletes it by its modification time; with 0 it is deleted at once. A download already gone counts as removed. Every download the pipeline removes, a put-aside one included, goes the same way.
