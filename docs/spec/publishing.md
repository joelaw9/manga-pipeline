# manga_pipeline spec — Publishing

Part of the spec; the index is [`SPEC.md`](../../SPEC.md). Update this file in the same change as the behaviour it describes.

## Publishing

The finished CBZ is written to a staging folder on the library's NFS export, outside every Kavita library root, then renamed into the series folder. Its `ComicInfo.xml` `Series` is the series' `ComicInfo.xml` name (a record series' from its record, an adopted series' from its files, a new series' its admitted name), which must be one of the series' Kavita names under Kavita's normalising (SPEC "State"), and its `LocalizedSeries` is Kavita's localized name (left out when Kavita has none). The rest of the download's `ComicInfo.xml` is kept where each field is valid for the schema, except what the pipeline writes itself (SPEC "Metadata", ComicInfo); `Number` comes from the chapter's name where the download has none, or no `ComicInfo.xml` at all. `Title` and `Volume` are the bulk converter's: the title is the download's `Title` without a leading label of the chapter's own number ("Chapter 4 : Where Is She?" → "Where Is She?"), else the name's title, with status markers (`[END]`, `(End of Season 1)`, …) stripped, and "Chapter <number>" when none is left (recorded as no title, as the baseline records an untitled bulk chapter, so the 2 compare equal); `Volume` is the chapter's volume below, and is left out when it has none.

A new series gets its own folder in the library folder of its source: `<library root>/<source folder>/<series>`. Its source folder is that of its link with the lowest manga id, else of its hand-placed folder: for a library manga, its source's download folder name (its display name made a valid file name, as Suwayomi names its download folder); for a hand-placed folder, its source folder (the first of its 2 folder names under `mangas`). A series whose manga's source is not installed is `needs_human` at admission, naming why and the fix (install the source; then `series new SERIES_ID`). A chapter of a series Kavita already knows goes into the series folder: the single folder that holds the series' files (SPEC "Services"), which must exist. Before a run publishes into a series Kavita holds, the folder Kavita's files of the series lie in must be the series' recorded folder; otherwise the series is `needs_human`, naming both, and nothing is published into it until a person links it again (`series link`, which records Kavita's folder) and retries it. A chapter is renamed into the series' folder as it is when it is published. The pipeline never creates a second folder for a series, and the program does not move series folders (the one-off move script `scripts/move_series_folder.py` aside).

A file the pipeline publishes is named as the bulk converter names its files: `Vol.<volume> Ch.<number> - <title>.cbz`, without `Vol.<volume> ` when the chapter has no volume and without ` - <title>` when it has no title or none is left once made safe. The title is made safe as the bulk converter does: ": " becomes " - ", the characters `\ / : * ? " < > |` become `_`, trailing dots and spaces go, and it is cut to 80 characters ending in "…".

A new chapter is named as the bulk converter names a chapter next to the series' recorded ones, with every published file and every open chapter of the series in view (chapters that arrive together see each other):

- Volume of a main chapter, from the source the series' bulk-converter record names as its volume basis (a new series' basis is chosen once, at validate, when none of its chapters is still at discover, over its chapters known then, by the bulk converter's order for a series it meets first: the first of `filename` (any main chapter's own volume), `season tags` (every chapter carries its season), `season markers` (a season-end marker), `<platform> seasons` (the series' official platform list's season ranges volume its main chapters), `mangadex` (MangaDex volumes 50 % or more of the main chapters), `override` (the override file volumes a main chapter), the side-story default (side stories and no main volume), else `none`): `filename` → the file's own volume (`Vol.N` in its name, else its `ComicInfo.xml` `Volume`); `season tags` → its season tag; `season markers` → its season, read from season-end and season-start markers in the chapters' titles (`ComicInfo.xml` `Title`, else the download's name) and from the record's own season volumes (each but the highest ends at its highest chapter; the highest stays open until a marker closes it), volumes numbered 1, 2, … in season order and the next one after the last end; `mangadex` → MangaDex's volume of its number (the stored aggregate); `override` → the series' override file `<paths.naming_overrides>/<series rules key>.yaml` (the name of the series' first folder, SPEC "Series identification"; `volumes: {1: "1-65", 2: "66-"}`; a chapter in no range has no volume; or `volume_starts: {1: 0, 2: 5, 3: 9}`: each volume's first chapter; a main chapter from a volume's start up to the next volume's start is in that volume, decimal parts included, the highest volume has no end, a chapter below the lowest start has no volume, and a start is written unpadded, as YAML reads `010` as 8); the side-story default → 1. `tapas seasons` and `webtoons seasons` → its season in the series' stored list of that platform (SPEC "Metadata", platform lists): volumes 1, 2, … in the list's season order, a chapter past the list in the next volume, one before its first season in volume 1; none when the list is not complete and chapters lie outside its seasons, or the series has no list of that platform. A new chapter the list puts past the main-story finale is a side story. The basis `none` gives no volume. Whatever the basis, a main chapter that carries no season and whose number lies in a range or a volume start's span of its series' override file gets that volume (the override file wins over every source). A hand edit of the chapter's volume or title wins over all of these (SPEC "Retroactive updates", hand edits).
- Gap fill, for the `filename`, `mangadex` and `none` bases: a main chapter the basis gives no volume takes the override file's volume (a `none` series whose override file re-volumed its recorded chapters follows it), then (`filename` only) MangaDex's, each from a source only when it gives the same volume as the basis on every chapter both cover, and only where the volume fits between its neighbours' (volumes never decrease along the chapter numbers); a source whose own fills are out of order fills nothing.
- A side story goes in the volume the series' side stories already have, else the volume after the highest main volume, else 2; side stories are numbered on from the highest published one, in the order (volume in its name, number, date, arrival); its title is `Side Story <n>: <title>` (the side tag and a bare chapter label stripped), else, when the series' platform list has exactly as many specials as the series has side stories (published and open), the n-th special's title, or `Side Story <n>` when none is left.
- A volume that is not a whole number (`ComicInfo.xml` `Volume` is an integer), from any source, a missing or malformed override file of an `override` series, and a malformed override file of any series (malformed also: both `volumes` and `volume_starts`; `volume_starts` whose volumes are not unquoted whole numbers from 0, whose lowest volume is not 0 or 1, that skips a volume (the reason names each missing one), or whose starts are not unquoted numbers from 0 rising with the volume) make the chapter `needs_human` (at the stage that names it: validate's checks, or comicinfo); a series with no override file and another basis has no override.
- An untitled main chapter takes its number's title in the series' platform list (cleaned as file titles are), else keeps "Chapter <number>"; MangaDex's chapter titles are not taken.

A published file whose chapter's volume later differs from the volume its name holds (none included) is renamed, with reading progress carried; a file the bulk converter named is renamed only when its chapter loses its volume altogether.

A library file is never overwritten. When the target name is taken, the chapter is `needs_human`, unless the staged file is gone and the target has the staged size: then the rename already happened (a crash after it) and publishing carries on. A published file recorded at a free target name describes a file that is gone: the new file's record replaces it.

Kavita scans are never requested back to back: a request made while a scan runs does not work. `export` requests no series scans; last in its run, after its publishes, its confirms and its new series' publishes, it requests at most 1 Kavita library scan, of `kavita.library_id`, when it published a file since the last request or chapters still wait on `kavita_scan`. A scan is awaited while chapters still wait on `kavita_scan` and its request is younger than `kavita.rescan_after_minutes`; while one is awaited no scan is requested, even after a publish: the new files wait for the next request. A request that fails is made by a later run. A chapter waits on `kavita_scan` until Kavita lists its file in the series with the size on disk and the series still exists. When a scan is requested while chapters still wait, each chapter that was already waiting when the previous request was made counts 1 attempt; a chapter past the attempt limit is `needs_human`. A series Kavita no longer holds is `needs_human`; its chapters keep waiting until a person links it again. A new series (admitted, no Kavita id yet) is published in a run where no scan is awaited, after that run's confirms, when none of its chapters is still on its way through `intake` or `process` (ready or claimed at a stage before publish; waiting and `needs_human` chapters aside) and its batch is open: nothing of it is published yet, or its publish has not been followed by a scan request. Its folder is created when missing; an existing one is used only when each `.cbz` in it is a file the series published or the file of one of its chapters at publish (a rename a crash left unrecorded), else the series is `needs_human`, naming the files. All its finished chapters are renamed in together, every new series ready in the run with them, and the run's 1 library scan indexes them. After that request it publishes nothing more until it has a Kavita id: chapters that finish meanwhile wait at publish. Its Kavita id is found once Kavita lists it: among the listed series no series row holds, those named as its ComicInfo `Series` under Kavita's name normalising, the one whose folder is the series' folder; it is looked for at most once per run, by whichever of confirm, the library sync or discovery asks first. Until it is found, its chapters wait on `kavita_scan` and are charged as any. Once found, the series is an ordinary series.

Once Kavita has indexed the file, the pipeline removes the CBZ from the downloads folder directly, not through Suwayomi, so Suwayomi keeps the chapter marked downloaded and does not fetch it again. With `export.retention_days` above 0, the CBZ is moved to `<paths.retention>/<source>/<title>/` (outside the downloads folder and every library root; when a file of its name is already there, under the first free name `<stem> (<n>)<suffix>`, n from 2, so both are kept) and touched, and the next `export` after the period deletes it by its modification time; with 0 it is deleted at once. A download already gone counts as removed. Every download the pipeline removes, a put-aside one included, goes the same way. A rejected or failed download (SPEC "Bad downloads") also goes the same way, and then Suwayomi's downloaded mark is cleared through Suwayomi, so the chapter can be downloaded again.
